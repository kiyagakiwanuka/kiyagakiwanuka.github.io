Portfolio Investment & Risk Management

BSc Economics · Techniques of Economic Analysis · University of Hertfordshire

5 stocks: Netflix, Amazon, Google, NVIDIA, RIO
2014–2018 returns
Efficient frontier with 10 allocations + Sharpe maximisation

Problem

How should capital be allocated across five major company shares to get the best risk-adjusted return?

Approach

  • Collected share data for Netflix, Amazon, Google, NVIDIA and RIO from 2014 to 2018.
  • Used matrix algebra to calculate the return and risk of an equally weighted portfolio.
  • Constructed an efficient frontier from 10 different allocation weights.
  • Used Excel Solver (linear programming) to find the allocation that maximises the Sharpe Ratio.

Findings

  • Visualised the risk/return trade-off across allocations with the efficient frontier.
  • Identified the Sharpe-maximising allocation as the optimal risk-adjusted portfolio.
