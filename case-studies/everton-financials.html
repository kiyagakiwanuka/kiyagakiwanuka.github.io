Business & Financial Performance Report: Everton FC

MSc Sport Business Management · Sheffield Hallam University

Five-year review, 2020–2025
ROCE, Current Ratio, Gearing
4,000-word corporate report

Problem

How financially healthy and structurally stable is Everton FC, given ownership changes, stadium construction and Premier League Profit & Sustainability Rules (PSR)?

Approach

  • Analysed five years of financial performance (2020–2025).
  • Calculated and benchmarked ratios including ROCE, Current Ratio and Gearing.
  • Evaluated ownership transitions, matchday ticket price inelasticity and wage-to-turnover trajectories.
  • Assessed capital efficiency relating to the new stadium construction.

Findings

  • Exposed core operating losses in the club's business.
  • Set out how competitive spending must be balanced with financial discipline under PSR, and how stadium commercialisation and private equity takeovers affect sustainability.
