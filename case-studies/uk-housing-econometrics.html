Time-series regression · ONS & Bank of England data

Econometric Analysis of UK Housing Market Determinants

BSc Economics · University of Hertfordshire

Quarterly data, 2005–2019
Regression model explaining 95% of price variation
Multicollinearity & heteroskedasticity tests

Problem

What drives UK house prices? The project tested how population density, interest rates and housing supply affect UK real estate prices.

Approach

  • Assembled quarter-by-quarter data from the Office for National Statistics (ONS) and the Bank of England, 2005–2019.
  • Estimated a time-series OLS regression of house prices on population density, interest rates and housing supply.
  • Tested model reliability by checking for multicollinearity and heteroskedasticity, and assessed statistical significance of each determinant.

Findings

  • The regression model explained 95% of the variation in prices.
  • Shows how external shifts such as interest rate changes feed into asset values through supply-and-demand equilibrium.
