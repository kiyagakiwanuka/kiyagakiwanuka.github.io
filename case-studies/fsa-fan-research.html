Non-League Football Marketing & Communication: "Who's Your Real Team?"

MSc Sport Business Management · Consulting report for the Football Supporters' Association · Lead Operations

12 regional clubs audited
Face-to-face interviews with fans & volunteers
Best Practice Toolkit for club executives

Problem

Why does online reach at non-league clubs fail to convert into gate attendance, and what low-cost actions can close that gap?

Approach

  • Ran primary face-to-face qualitative interviews with fans and volunteers.
  • Carried out cross-platform digital audits across twelve regional clubs.
  • Deep-dived the operations of Worsbrough Bridge AFC, Maltby Main FC and Penistone Church FC to isolate the "digital-to-physical action gap".
  • Thematically coded Sheffield FC primary data into themes such as "Grassroots Intimacy" vs "Professional Disillusionment", using the AIDA model and Technology Acceptance Model, and integrated it with quantitative ground-experience scores.

Findings

  • Identified where online reach fails to convert into attendance.
  • Delivered a low-cost Best Practice Toolkit: brand perception audits, mobile-first design optimisation and tactical scheduling to attract disillusioned Football League fans.
