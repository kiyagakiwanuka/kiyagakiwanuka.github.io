Feasibility Study: Over-40s Basketball Programme, B. Braun Sheffield Sharks

MSc Sport Business Management · Sheffield Hallam University · Sole consultant

97 eligible survey responses
Break-even tested at 6 price points × 2 cost scenarios
20,000 randomised reweightings in sensitivity test

Problem

A Super League Basketball club asked whether it could sustainably run a weekly recreational session for adults over 40, and whether to fund it through a Sport England grant or through participant fees.

Approach

  • Designed and distributed a primary survey (97 eligible responses) and interviewed the founder of the only comparable provider in Sheffield.
  • Benchmarked court hire, sessional coaching and affiliation costs across the city.
  • Sized the addressable market from ONS Census 2021 data.
  • Traced the club's corporate structure through Companies House filings to establish which entity could apply for public money.
  • Built a contribution-margin model testing break-even across six price points and two cost scenarios.
  • Ranked the two funding pathways with weighted multi-criteria decision analysis, stress-tested against 20,000 randomised reweightings of the criteria.

Findings

  • The survey reached lapsed and current players, not the inactive adults Sport England funds: 85.6% had played basketball before and only 6.2% recorded a week without a single 30-minute bout of activity.
  • This contradicted the premise of the brief. The sampling limitation became the basis of the recommendation: launch commercially for the audience the evidence found, and defer the grant application to year two once a running pilot can evidence the reach a funder scores against.
  • Delivered a 6,000-word report with a costed launch plan, a risk register and a staged measurement framework.
