### 19. Feasibility Study for an Over-40s Recreational Basketball Programme – B. Braun Sheffield Sharks
* **Summary of Project**: Worked as sole consultant to a Super League Basketball club on a live commercial question: could it sustainably run a weekly recreational session for adults over 40, and should it fund that session through a Sport England grant or through participant fees. Designed and distributed a primary survey returning 97 eligible responses, interviewed the founder of the only comparable provider in Sheffield, and benchmarked court hire, sessional coaching and affiliation costs across the city. Sized the addressable market from ONS Census 2021 data, traced the club’s corporate structure through Companies House filings to establish which of its entities could apply for public money, and built a contribution-margin model testing break-even across six price points and two cost scenarios. Ranked the two funding pathways using weighted multi-criteria decision analysis, then stress-tested that ranking against 20,000 randomised reweightings of the criteria. Delivered a 6,000-word report carrying a costed launch plan, a risk register and a staged measurement framework.
* **Why the Project is Useful and Relevant**: The survey reached lapsed and current players rather than the inactive adults Sport England funds: 85.6% had played basketball before, and only 6.2% recorded a week without a single 30-minute bout of activity. That contradicted the premise the brief started from. I made the sampling limitation the basis of the recommendation, advising the club to launch commercially for the audience the evidence found and to defer the grant application to year two, once a running pilot could evidence the reach a funder scores against. Demonstrates the ability to test a client’s preferred route against primary data, tell them it fails, and hand back a costed alternative they can act on.
* **Skills Gained**: Primary Research Design (survey construction and semi-structured interviewing), Financial Modelling & Break-Even Analysis, Multi-Criteria Decision Analysis with Sensitivity Testing, Corporate Due Diligence (Companies House, funding eligibility), Client-Facing Consultancy Reporting.
