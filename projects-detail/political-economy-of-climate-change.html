Political Economy of Climate Change: Climate Justice in Bangladesh and Tuvalu

OverviewMethodologyFindingsWhy it mattersSkills gained

Overview

Analyzed climate justice and the economic responsibilities of developed nations toward developing ones.

Methodology & process

  1. Evaluated the "loss and damage" framework and examined the political barriers and long-term costs associated with climate-induced migration.

Findings & outcomes

  • Focuses on the ethical and environmental dimensions of economics.

Why it matters

It builds an understanding of economic externalities (how actions of one group harm others) which is highly valuable for ESG consulting and international development roles.
