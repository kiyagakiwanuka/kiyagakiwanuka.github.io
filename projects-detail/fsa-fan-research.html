Overview

Served as Lead Operations for a consulting report prepared for the Football Supporters' Association (FSA).

Context & question

Why does online reach at non-league clubs fail to convert into gate attendance, and what low-cost actions can close that gap?

Methodology & process

  1. Ran primary face-to-face qualitative interviews with fans and volunteers.
  2. Carried out cross-platform digital audits across twelve regional clubs.
  3. Deep-dived the operations of Worsbrough Bridge AFC, Maltby Main FC and Penistone Church FC to isolate the "digital-to-physical action gap".
  4. Thematically coded Sheffield FC primary data into themes such as "Grassroots Intimacy" vs "Professional Disillusionment", using the AIDA model and Technology Acceptance Model, and integrated it with quantitative ground-experience scores.

Data & tools

AIDA modelTechnology Acceptance Modelthematic analysis

Findings & outcomes

  • Delivered a low-cost Best Practice Toolkit: brand perception audits, mobile-first design optimisation and tactical scheduling to attract disillusioned Football League fans.

Why it matters

Provides highly practical, evidence-based solutions for community sports clubs operating on thin budgets.

It demonstrates how applying core consumer behavior frameworks (like the AIDA model and the Technology Acceptance Model) can eliminate user friction, optimize local marketing funnels, and build long-term fan loyalty without substantial capital expenditure.
