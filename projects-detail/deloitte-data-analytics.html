Overview

Completed a hands-on simulation focused on data analysis, data modeling, and forensic technology.

Context & question

How do you turn raw corporate records into a classified, trustworthy dataset - and then into a dashboard someone can use?

Key concepts

  • Data classification: Assigning records to consistent categories so that they can be counted, compared and audited.
  • Data modelling: Structuring data into related tables and fields so that analysis tools can aggregate it correctly.
  • Forensic technology: Using data analysis to detect anomalies, irregularities or risk in corporate records.
  • Interactive dashboards: Filters and drill-downs that let users explore the data themselves rather than read a static report.

Methodology & process

  1. Structured the data for analysis.
  2. Built an interactive visualisation dashboard in Tableau.

Why it matters

Builds technical proficiency in industry-standard business intelligence tools, showing an ability to clean complex datasets and structure them into clear visual assets that solve business and technological problems.

The clean-model-visualise workflow is the daily work of audit analytics, BI and consulting data teams.
