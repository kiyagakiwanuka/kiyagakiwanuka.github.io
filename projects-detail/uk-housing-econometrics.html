Econometric Analysis of UK Housing Market Determinants

Overview

Conducted a time-series regression analysis to identify how population density, interest rates, and housing supply affect UK real estate prices.

Methodology & process

  1. Assembled quarter-by-quarter data from the Office for National Statistics (ONS) and the Bank of England, 2005–2019.
  2. Tested model reliability by checking for multicollinearity and heteroskedasticity, and assessed statistical significance of each determinant.

Data & tools

Office for National Statistics (ONS)Bank of Englandregression

Findings & outcomes

  • The regression model explained 95% of the variation in prices.
  • Shows how external shifts such as interest rate changes feed into asset values through supply-and-demand equilibrium.

Why it matters

Demonstrates a strong ability to handle complex time-series data and extract meaningful trends from national databases.
