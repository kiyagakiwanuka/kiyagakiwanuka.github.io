Overview

Analyzed the evolution of global production, focusing on the rise of Global Value Chains (GVCs) and "trade in tasks." Evaluated the "smile curve" concept to show how value is distributed across production stages.

Methodology & process

  1. Investigated the "middle-income trap," Foreign Direct Investment (FDI), and the impact of global shocks like COVID-19.

Data & tools

smile curve

Findings & outcomes

  • Provides a deep understanding of how the global economy is organized through fragmented production networks.

Why it matters

Helps identify where value is created in a supply chain, which is essential for global supply chain management and market entry strategy.
