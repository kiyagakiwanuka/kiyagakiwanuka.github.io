Overview

Advised a hypothetical consumer goods client on multi-channel digital advertising performance.

Context & question

Which audiences and channels are actually converting - and where should the advertising budget go next?

Key concepts

  • Conversion rate: The share of an audience completing a desired action. Comparing it across channels and segments shows where spend works.
  • Multi-channel attribution: Customers often see several channels before converting, so judging each channel's contribution requires care.
  • Segmentation: Splitting customers into audiences reveals differences that an overall average hides.

Methodology & process

  1. Clarified the client's goals before touching the data.
  2. Interpreted simulated customer behaviour data in Excel by audience and channel.
  3. Compared conversion trends across segments to identify strong and weak performers.
  4. Framed data-backed recommendations aligned to the client's objectives.

Data & tools

Excel

Why it matters

Replicates the day-to-day workflow of a corporate Marketing Analytics team.

It mirrors the weekly rhythm of a marketing analytics team: segment, compare, recommend.
