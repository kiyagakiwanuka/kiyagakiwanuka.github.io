Assessing the Consequences of Artificial Intelligence on the Demand for Labour

OverviewMethodologyDataFindingsWhy it mattersSkills gained

Overview

Evaluated the impact of AI on the labor market by focusing on overall employment levels and changing skill demands.

Methodology & process

  1. Applied the Task-Based Model (Acemoglu and Restrepo) to distinguish between the displacement effect, productivity effect, and reinstatement effect.
  2. Produced a critical essay supported by a structured argument map.

Data & tools

Task-Based Modelargument map

Findings & outcomes

  • Applies advanced economic theory to modern technological shifts.

Why it matters

It moves beyond simple job-loss narratives to understand how technology reshapes labor demand at a granular level, which is valuable for strategic workforce consulting.
