Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights Job Simulation

OverviewContextConceptsMethodologyDataWhy it mattersSkills gained

Overview

Developed business intelligence visuals for executive leadership at Tata Consultancy Services.

Context & question

What does an executive need to see - and what should be asked before they see it?

Key concepts

  • Data storytelling: Choosing the chart, ordering and annotation so that the key message is obvious to a time-poor audience.
  • Chart selection: Matching the visual to the question (trend, comparison, composition, distribution) to avoid misleading reads.
  • Requirements framing: Drafting the business questions first ensures that the visuals answer what the client actually needs to decide.

Methodology & process

  1. Drafted strategic questions to prepare for a client alignment meeting.
  2. Cleaned retail revenue datasets.
  3. Built executive-level visuals in Tableau and Power BI to answer those questions.

Data & tools

PowerBITableau

Why it matters

Focuses on turning complex operational numbers into impactful, executive-ready visual stories using Tableau and PowerBI, ensuring data analytics directly supports long-term corporate decision-making.

Executive reporting succeeds or fails on clarity. This simulation practises building visuals for a senior audience under a consulting brief.
