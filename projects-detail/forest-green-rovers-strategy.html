Strategic and Marketing Plan for Forest Green Rovers F.C.

Overview

Created a 4,000-word strategic roadmap to help a sports organization build a sustainable competitive advantage.

Context & question

How can a club with a distinctive identity turn that identity into a lasting competitive advantage and commercial growth?

Key concepts

  • PESTEL: Scans the Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental and Legal forces acting on an organisation.
  • Porter's Five Forces: Assesses industry attractiveness through rivalry, the threat of new entrants and substitutes, and buyer and supplier power.
  • VRIO: Tests internal resources for Value, Rarity, Inimitability and Organisation; only resources meeting all four sustain a competitive advantage.
  • STP: Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning: divide the market, choose which segments to serve, and define how the brand should be perceived by them.

Methodology & process

  1. Mapped the external environment with PESTEL and Porter's Five Forces.
  2. Audited internal resources with VRIO to identify which could underpin sustained advantage.
  3. Built the marketing plan through STP, linking chosen segments to the club's environmental positioning.

Why it matters

Bridges high-level business management theory with practical sports marketing.

It demonstrates how a brand can leverage core values (such as environmental sustainability) to drive fan engagement, media communication decisions, and commercial growth.

Strategy and brand roles in any industry use the same sequence: understand the environment, identify defensible resources, then target the customers who value them. This project runs the full sequence end to end.
