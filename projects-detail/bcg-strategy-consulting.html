Overview

Applied structured creative thinking techniques to challenge underlying business assumptions for a luxury clothing brand.

Context & question

Which assumptions is a luxury clothing brand making about its revenue - and what happens if they are challenged?

Key concepts

  • Assumption busting: Listing the beliefs a business takes for granted and deliberately inverting them to open new options.
  • Structured brainstorming: Facilitation techniques that generate many ideas before evaluating them, then filter for commercial viability.
  • Repositioning: Changing how a brand is perceived or which customers it targets in order to unlock growth.

Methodology & process

  1. Facilitated brainstorming on revenue optimisation and market repositioning.
  2. Screened the ideas for commercial viability.

Why it matters

Introduces the consulting workflow and mindset required at top-tier firms.

It builds the capacity to look at a business from a fresh perspective, balancing creative ideation with commercial viability.

Consulting and strategy roles reward people who can question a client's frame without losing commercial realism.
